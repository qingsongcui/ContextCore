## Dependency distributions

Python installations retain dependency notices in their installed package metadata. A built browser distribution must carry the installed runtime dependencies' license texts alongside its compiled assets. Dependency audit results are separate from copyright/license notices and do not establish legal clearance or trademark ownership.

Optional semantic-model downloads are not bundled or authorized by building the app. Their pinned model/runtime plans and associated upstream licenses must remain visible before a user chooses to prepare them.
